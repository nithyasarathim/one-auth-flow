Trust checks & token anatomy

  • Access token: JWT HS256 · sub=userId · client_id · type:"access_token" · iss:"one-auth" · aud=client_id · 24 h.
  • Auth code: 32 random bytes, 60 s, deleted on first exchange (replay-proof).
  • Allow-lists: client_id and exact redirect_uri checked at /sso/authorize and again at /sso/token.
  • client_secret never touches the browser — only the relying app's backend calls /sso/token.
  • API → OneMail: HMAC-SHA256(to:otp:timestamp), max age 2 min (prod) / 10 min (dev), timingSafeEqual.
  • Rate limits per IP+path per window: authorize 20 · token 20 · userinfo 30 · login 8 · verify-email 3.
